About me

I am currently an Image Quality Research Engineer at MediaTek Inc. I also serve as a Research Assistant in Computational Photograph Lab at National Yang Ming Chiao Tung University, supervised by Prof. Yu-Lun Liu. My research lies at the intersection of computational photography and low-level computer vision, with a dedicated focus on color constancy and image quality/aesthetic assessment. I received my Master’s degree from the Graduate Institute of Communication Engineering at National Taiwan University, advised by Prof. Jian-Jiun Ding. Prior to joining MediaTek Inc., I was a Camera Imaging Engineer Intern at Logitech and an Image Processing Engineer Intern at Robert BOSCH GmbH.

Overview

One panorama, one reproducible photograph

PhotoGuider turns a full 360° environment into a selected viewpoint, a refined crop, camera parameters, and an explanation grounded in measured photographic attributes.

120candidate views24 pan positions × 5 tilt positions
60°working viewComposition is refined after direction selection
3camera parameterspan, tilt, and field of view
4assessment tokensSubject, Composition, Integrity, Aesthetics

Method

From viewing direction to explained framing

The system separates where to look from how to frame, then exposes the photographic evidence behind its final decision.

S

Subject

How clearly the main visual subject is identified and emphasized.

C

Composition

Balance, placement, symmetry, leading lines, and spatial organization.

I

Integrity

Whether important content remains complete and avoids unintended truncation.

A

Aesthetics

The visual appeal of the selected view as a complete photograph.
